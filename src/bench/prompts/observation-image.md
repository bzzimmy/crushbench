Each turn you receive a screenshot of the game. The board is the 9×9 grid of candies; striped candies have stripes, wrapped candies have a wrapped-sweet look, and colour bombs are dark balls with sprinkles. The timer and level are on the tags to the left. Count columns and rows carefully from the top-left corner to address a cell. Alongside the image you get the score, the level, how many candies this level still needs, and the seconds left on the clock as text.
